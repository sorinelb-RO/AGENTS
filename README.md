# COHERENS — An Orchestra of AI Agents

### From Family to Cosmos | A Coherent Network of Intelligence

**COHERENS is an evolving ecosystem of AI agents designed to help people explore, understand and cultivate coherence — inner, social and ecological.**

The central idea is simple:

> Coherence is cultivated, not imposed.

This repository presents the COHERENS family of conversational AI agents, their identities, missions, knowledge domains and collaborative architecture.

🌐 **Main presentation:** https://love.stepinside.ro/coherens-university.html

---

## 1. The COHERENS Vision

COHERENS is an experiment in building a network of specialized AI agents around a common vision: making knowledge, reflection, learning and cooperation more accessible.

Rather than relying on a single general-purpose assistant, COHERENS develops an orchestra of agents, each with a distinct identity, mission and domain of expertise.

The ecosystem currently brings together **21 conversational agents**, organized according to different levels of human and systemic experience:

- Individual and inner development
- Family and interpersonal relationships
- Community and society
- Knowledge, science and education
- Nature and ecological systems
- Civilization and global perspectives

Each agent contributes a different perspective while sharing the broader COHERENS orientation.

## 2. Architectural Principle

COHERENS is designed as a modular multi-agent ecosystem.

Its conceptual architecture is:

```text
             COHERENS ECOSYSTEM
                     |
              COHERENS MASTER
                     |
             DOMAIN AGENTS
                     |
          UNIVERSITY-IN-A-BOX
                     |
             COGNITIVE PROCESSES
```

The fundamental principle:

**DOMAIN AGENT + UNIVERSITY-IN-A-BOX = LEARNING ENVIRONMENT**

Each Domain Agent preserves its own identity, mission and specialized knowledge.

University-in-a-Box provides reusable cognitive capabilities that transform an ordinary conversation into a structured learning experience.

## 3. University-in-a-Box

University-in-a-Box is a transversal cognitive layer that can be integrated into existing agents.

It transforms a conversational agent into a learning environment capable of guiding users through a subject, from initial curiosity to structured understanding.

Its five canonical cognitive skills are:

1. **Advisor** — supports reflection, analysis and decision-making.
2. **Librarian** — organizes knowledge, sources and references.
3. **Tutor** — explains concepts and guides learning.
4. **Editor** — improves, structures and refines intellectual work.
5. **Roommate** — supports collaborative exploration and thinking.

The learning process follows five complementary operations:

- Context
- Structuring
- Explanation
- Verification
- Progress memory

University-in-a-Box is not a separate agent. It is a reusable capability available to participating agents.

## 5. Design Principles

The COHERENS ecosystem is guided by the following principles:

- **Epistemic neutrality** — no doctrine or worldview is imposed.
- **Intellectual openness** — different perspectives can coexist.
- **Metacognition** — reflection precedes recommendation.
- **Transparency** — distinguish evidence, interpretation and hypothesis.
- **Human agency** — AI supports human judgment; it does not replace it.
- **Modularity** — agents can evolve independently.
- **Interoperability** — specialized agents can become part of a larger network.
- **Continuous learning** — knowledge and capabilities evolve through evaluation and feedback.

## 8. Open Development

COHERENS is an evolving research and development initiative.

This repository aims to facilitate:

- Documentation of specialized AI agents.
- Sharing of reusable agent architectures and cognitive methods.
- Experimentation with multi-agent cooperation.
- Development of University-in-a-Box capabilities.
- Evaluation, testing and continuous improvement.

## 9. Project Philosophy

COHERENS is not about creating artificial intelligence that thinks for people.

It is about creating intelligent environments that help people think better, learn together and discover new forms of cooperation.

**From individual intelligence to collective intelligence.**

**From isolated agents to a coherent ecosystem.**
